01The Mark

An asymmetric four-curve dipole. Two charged poles — salmon and lime — connected by field lines that bend through the space between them. The asymmetry is intentional: real field lines aren't perfect mirror images, and neither is ours.

03Clear Space & Sizing

Give the lockup room to breathe. The minimum padding around the lockup on all sides equals the height of the mark itself.

Minimum Sizes

  • Mark only · with poles colored: 32px wide. Below this, the asymmetric curves blur into each other; the colored poles still convey the brand.
  • Mark only · mono: 24px wide. Below this, the field-line silhouette becomes unclear.
  • Horizontal lockup: 200px wide minimum. Below this, the wordmark "field" loses readability.
  • Stacked lockup: 96px wide minimum. Below this, prefer the mark alone.

04Color System

Four color families. Salmon (#FF6B6B) is the brand's primary accent — it dominates wherever color appears. Lime (#BEF264) is the offset, used sparingly at deliberate moments. Ink and bone do all the structural work.

★ lime-500 is the offset accent. Use sparingly — at the right pole of the mark, on hover/active states for featured cards, on accent ticks in charts. Never as a primary element of a button, never as a section background, never on its own as the brand color. Stays out of warning UI states (yellow conventionally signals warnings; reserve that semantic).

The Discipline Rule

Salmon dominates wherever brand color appears. Lime appears only at deliberate moments. A user should be able to scroll through five dashboard screens and see plenty of salmon, with lime visible only at specific beats. If lime starts showing up everywhere, the system is broken.

05Typography

The wordmark uses General Sans Medium (500). The wordmark is rendered as paths in all SVG files — you don't need General Sans installed to use them.

field
General Sans · weight 500 · letter-spacing -0.025em · lowercase

UI & Body Copy

  • Wordmark: General Sans Medium (500), lowercase, tight letter-spacing.
  • Headlines: Inter Light (300) or ExtraLight (200) at large sizes. General Sans also works.
  • Body: Inter Regular (400) at 14–16px, line-height 1.55.
  • Labels & UI chrome: Inter Medium (500), small (11px), often uppercase with 1.5–2px letter-spacing.
  • Code & data: SF Mono / Menlo / Liberation Mono fallbacks.

07Don't

Things that break the system.

✕
Don't recolor the mark
Salmon stays #FF6B6B. Lime stays #BEF264. Don't substitute different oranges, reds, or greens.
✕
Don't add gradients or shadows
No gradients, drop shadows, glows, 3D effects, or treatments. The mark is flat by design.
✕
Don't put the mark on busy backgrounds
Use ink or bone backgrounds only. Photos, gradients, and patterns kill the mark's clarity.
✕
Don't squish, stretch, or rotate
The mark's 2:1 aspect ratio is part of its identity. The asymmetric curves are tuned. Don't deform.
✕
Don't introduce a third accent
Salmon + lime is the system. Adding a third color makes it look like a kid's brand or a 90s startup.
✕
Don't recreate the mark from scratch
Always use the supplied SVG. The asymmetric curve placements aren't a free design choice.
✕
Don't capitalize the wordmark
It's "field" lowercase, always. "Field" or "FIELD" looks wrong against the lowercase brand identity.
✕
Don't use lime as a primary color
Lime is the offset accent — used at moments, not as the brand. If lime is dominating, salmon should be.
